Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 09, 2026

Welcome to SoulMerge ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the SoulMerge mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use SoulMerge, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture provided by your Google account.
  • Profile Information: Information you choose to add to your dating profile, such as your bio, age, gender, interests, and profile photographs.
  • Communications & Interactions: Connection requests, chat messages, and token usage activities within the application.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: When you purchase tokens, payments are processed securely through certified third-party payment gateways (such as PayHere). We do not store your credit card or bank credentials on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and improve our services, including to:

  • Create and manage your user account and profile.
  • Match you with other users based on your selected interests and preferences.
  • Enable connection requests, real-time messaging, and interactive features.
  • Manage token balances and process in-app purchase verifications.
  • Maintain app security, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms of service.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase with database-level Row Level Security (RLS). All communications between the mobile application and backend servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/HTTPS protocols.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Other Users: Information on your public profile (such as photos, display name, and bio) is visible to other users of SoulMerge.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as Supabase for database hosting and PayHere for payment processing) who adhere to strict data security standards.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes.

5. Age Restriction (18+)

SoulMerge is strictly designed and intended for users who are at least 18 years of age (or the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will take immediate steps to delete their data.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have full control over your personal data. You can edit your profile details directly in the app at any time. If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data permanently, you may request deletion from within the app settings or by contacting our support team at the email address below. Upon confirmation, your data will be permanently purged from our active databases.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
